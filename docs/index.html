📱 About Passandi

Passandi is a secure password manager that keeps your sensitive data encrypted and accessible only to you. All your vault data is encrypted locally with AES-256-CBC, and your encryption key is derived from your PIN — not from your account ID.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Passandi ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how your data is handled.

Data We Collect

  • Account Information — email address (for authentication)
  • Vault Data — passwords, notes, and other items you store (all encrypted client-side)
  • Usage Data — anonymous analytics via PostHog (page views, feature usage)
  • Crash Reports — via Sentry (app errors only, no personal data)

How We Protect Your Data

  • All vault data is encrypted with AES-256-CBC before leaving your device
  • Your PIN is hashed with SHA-256 with a unique salt
  • We never have access to your master encryption key
  • Data in transit uses HTTPS/TLS

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — authentication and encrypted cloud sync
  • Sentry — crash reporting (opt-in)
  • PostHog — product analytics (anonymous)

Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app (Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account).
